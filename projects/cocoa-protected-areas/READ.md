# Cocoa Plantations and Protected-Area Monitoring

## Interactive Geospatial Decision-Support Application

This project presents an interactive Google Earth Engine application for exploring the spatial distribution of cocoa plantations and their relationship with protected areas.

The application demonstrates how satellite-derived geospatial information can support sustainable cocoa production, protected-area monitoring, conservation planning, supply-chain risk screening, and environmental decision-making.

## Launch the Live Application

[Open the Cocoa Plantations and Protected Areas application](https://abuitohanosa.users.earthengine.app/view/cocoa-plantations-and-their-implications-on-protected-areas)

## Decision Problem

Cocoa production supports rural livelihoods and regional economies. However, the expansion of cocoa cultivation can increase pressure on forests, biodiversity, and protected landscapes.

Government agencies, conservation organizations, cocoa-sector companies, and sustainability teams need accessible tools to investigate questions such as:

- Where are cocoa plantations spatially concentrated?
- Where are mapped cocoa-growing areas located near or within protected areas?
- Which locations may require additional monitoring or field verification?
- How can satellite information support sustainable cocoa sourcing?
- How can complex geospatial results be communicated to nontechnical stakeholders?

This application provides an interactive, landscape-level screening tool for exploring these questions.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to transform geospatial information on cocoa plantations and protected areas into an accessible decision-support application.

The application is designed to support:

- environmental monitoring;
- sustainable agricultural planning;
- conservation prioritization;
- cocoa supply-chain assessment;
- stakeholder communication; and
- identification of locations that may require further investigation.

## Application Features

The application allows users to:

- explore mapped cocoa plantation areas;
- display protected-area boundaries;
- compare cocoa distribution with conservation areas;
- turn relevant map layers on and off;
- zoom to locations of interest;
- examine spatial patterns interactively; and
- communicate analytical results through a publicly accessible web map.

## Analytical Workflow

The project followed these main steps:

1. Relevant cocoa plantation and protected-area datasets were prepared.
2. The datasets were imported and processed in Google Earth Engine.
3. Cocoa plantation areas were visualized across the study area.
4. Protected-area boundaries were added as conservation reference layers.
5. The spatial relationship between cocoa cultivation and protected areas was examined.
6. An interactive Google Earth Engine application was developed.
7. The application was deployed publicly for stakeholder access.

## Responsible Interpretation

This application is intended as a landscape-level screening and decision-support tool.

Mapped cocoa areas should not automatically be interpreted as evidence of illegal activity or attributed to individual farmers, farms, communities, or companies.

Results may be affected by the dates, spatial resolution, classification accuracy, and completeness of the underlying datasets. Locations identified through the application should therefore be interpreted as areas that may warrant additional assessment.

Regulatory, enforcement, investment, or conservation decisions should incorporate field verification, current administrative information, local knowledge, and other independent evidence.
